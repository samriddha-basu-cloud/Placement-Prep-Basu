# Expansion Log (notes 111-227)

[[00 Home]]

This edition adds **117** notes to the original 110. Notes 001-110 are unchanged apart from a short *Go deeper* link list at the end of each note, two small corrections in [[080 SAP MM — Materials Management]] and [[084 SAP QM & PM]], and navigation links. The deepest additions are Supply Chain Management (35 notes), then SAP ERP, Operations Management and Statistics.

**How the notes were built:** each was drafted from the standard syllabus plus current public sources, with worked numbers computed in Python. News boxes cite pages that were opened during writing, but web research was rate-limited partway through, so figures marked *recalled*, *from memory*, *illustrative* or *verify* in a note should be re-checked before you quote them in an interview. Time-sensitive facts (GST, tariffs, SAP maintenance dates, RBI rates, FTA status) carry the date they were checked (early October 2026).
